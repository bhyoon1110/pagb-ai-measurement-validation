# Suggested manuscript wording

The separate, access-controlled provenance archive contains the five trained checkpoint files and field-level records used to verify the final run. That archive is not included in either the manuscript review/submission package or the public repository. The evaluation code, non-identifying provenance summaries, aggregate result tables, and analysis figures are available as release v1.0.0 at https://github.com/bhyoon1110/pagb-ai-measurement-validation. Raw optical micrographs, manual annotations, cached probability maps, field-level manifests, and trained checkpoint files are not publicly distributed because their release requires institutional data-permission review; subject to those permissions, requests may be directed to the corresponding author.
